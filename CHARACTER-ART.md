# New character artwork

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Existing characters-cutout-v2.png was used only as a style reference. Final project assets are in dist/assets/character-*.png.

Shared prompt: single full-body transparent game cutout; cute but slightly annoying fictional chibi comic character; warm uneven dark brown pencil outlines, soft muted watercolor and colored-pencil fills, peach cheek circles, cream skin and simple eyes. Centered square, full feet visible, generous transparent margins. No background, ground, text, watermark or border.

Subjects:
- husband: striped home shirt, slippers, TV remote, mildly smug
- wife: bob haircut, cardigan, trousers, smartphone, raised eyebrow
- mother-in-law: older woman, permed gray hair, glasses, cardigan, hands on hips
- ceo: dark suit, side-parted hair, coffee, pointing at wristwatch
- computer-error: anthropomorphic monitor, error cross, little arms and legs, crooked keyboard
- president: silver hair, navy suit, neutral gold pin, plain podium
- politician: glasses, dark hair, beige suit, green tie, blank sheet, reassuring gesture

President and politician are fictional and must not resemble actual people or use party/flag symbols.
